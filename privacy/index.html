Who we are

Javia Ventures is a web studio based in Tiruchirappalli, Tamil Nadu, India, run by Jayaprakash. We build websites for roofing contractors in the United States. Contact: jayaprakash@getjaviaventures.com.

What we collect

  • When you request a demo: your name, company name, email, and optionally your phone number and website or city.
  • When you email us: your email address and whatever you write.
  • Public business information: to prepare homepage concepts and outreach, we use information businesses publish themselves, such as their name, phone number, city, website and public Google rating.

This site does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

  • To build and send your homepage demo, and to reply to you.
  • To deliver the website and services you ask us for.

We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't add you to newsletters.

Services we use

  • Google Fonts for the typefaces on this site.

Preview pages

Your choices
